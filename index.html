Vacuum Chlorination System

Explore Products

About the System

A vacuum chlorination system uses a vacuum-based mechanism to draw and meter chlorine gas into a water stream for disinfection. System design and components vary by manufacturer, capacity and application.

Our Products

Browse the components below. Select “View Details” to read a short description.

01 · VACUUM COMPONENT

Ejector

Uses a motive-water stream to create vacuum and draw chlorine gas into the water flow.

Commonly based on a venturi principle. Confirm water pressure, flow requirements and material compatibility from the selected model's datasheet.
02 · MEASUREMENT

Flow Meter

Indicates the chlorine-gas flow rate in compatible chlorination equipment.

Flow range, calibration, scale units and gas compatibility depend on the exact meter model.
03 · VACUUM CONTROL

Vacuum Regulator

Helps regulate vacuum and control gas supply in a designed vacuum chlorination assembly.

Construction, operating range and safety features must be checked against manufacturer documentation.
04 · PRESSURE CONTROL

Pressure Reducing Valve

Reduces inlet pressure to a specified downstream pressure in applications for which the valve is designed.

Do not assume every pressure reducing valve is suitable for chlorine service. Verify the exact gas, pressure rating and materials.
05 · MONITORING

Vacuum Switch

Provides an electrical switching signal when the sensed vacuum reaches its configured threshold.

Check switching range, electrical rating, connection type and compatibility with the control system.
06 · FLOW CONTROL

Auxiliary Valve

An additional valve used for an intended isolation or control function in a compatible assembly.

Its exact role and permitted use depend on the equipment design and manufacturer instructions.
07 · MOUNTING

Clamp

Secures suitable pipes, tubing or components in a mounting arrangement.

Select a clamp based on dimensions, load, environment and the manufacturer's installation guidance.
08 · CHLORINATION

Floor-Mounted Chlorinator

A floor-installed chlorinator assembly designed to meter chlorine for a water-treatment process.

Capacity, mounting, gas supply configuration and required accessories depend on the selected model.
09 · GAS DISTRIBUTION

Manifold

Connects multiple gas-cylinder outlets to a common header in a properly engineered system.

A chlorine-service manifold requires approved materials, connections, pressure ratings and professional installation.
10 · PIPING

Copper Pipe

Listed for comparison as a piping component; suitability must be assessed for the intended service.

Copper must not be assumed suitable for chlorine gas. Verify dry or wet service conditions and the equipment manufacturer's approved materials before specifying any pipe.

Applications

Drinking Water

Chlorination may be used as part of a properly designed drinking-water disinfection process.

Wastewater

Selected treatment plants use controlled disinfection as one stage of wastewater treatment.

Industrial Water

Suitable systems can support process-water treatment when selected and operated to relevant requirements.
